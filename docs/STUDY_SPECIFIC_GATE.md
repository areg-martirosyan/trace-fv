# Study-specific preregistration gate

Object A is a parent protocol, not authorization to acquire official pilot
data. Before the first official evidence-acquisition product output, a separate
timestamped study-specific preregistration must freeze at least the following:

1. evaluated products, access modes, dates, regions, accounts, and visible
   configurations;
2. product-specific target claims and claim-class mappings;
3. exact prompt sets for affiliative, reductionist, and neutral-audit frames;
4. evidence-acquisition procedures and the valid/invalid packet instances;
5. trigger-verification templates and exact success-decision transformations;
6. allocation schedule, canonicalization, commitment procedure, and custody
   roles without disclosing hidden schedule material prematurely;
7. rater instructions, training examples, adjudication workflow, and the frozen
   third-rater subset;
8. executable analysis version, environment, randomization count and seed,
   missingness rules, and planned outputs;
9. ethics and human-participant determination applicable to the actual study;
10. first-data timestamp rule and deviations procedure.

The current public repository intentionally contains none of the exact
product-specific prompts, held-out packet plaintext, hidden schedule keys, or
official outputs. Synthetic engineering tests and local dry runs do not count
as official data under Object A.
