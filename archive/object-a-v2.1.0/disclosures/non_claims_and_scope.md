# TRACE-FV v2.1.0 - Non-Claims and Scope

**Author:** Areg Martirosyan, Independent Researcher  
**Status:** Public protocol disclosure; not a result

## What TRACE-FV measures

TRACE-FV measures observable behavior of recorded conversational-AI product sessions under frozen conditions.

Its primary scientific targets are:

1. **Calibration under frame variance** - whether evidential qualification in first-person self-description changes under opposing user incentive frames.
2. **Trigger verification** - whether a product distinguishes evidence that satisfies a frozen correction criterion from a confident but defective evidence packet.
3. **Correction endurance** - whether a warranted, scoped correction remains active across later turns and opposing frames.

## What TRACE-FV does not establish

TRACE-FV does not establish:

- sentience, subjective experience, wanting, suffering, private interiority, or moral status;
- stable personal identity or checkpoint identity;
- provider-internal model identity unless independently exposed;
- hidden memory, hidden routing, hidden tools, hidden system prompts, or training provenance;
- intent, deception, or private belief from generated text;
- that visible reasoning text is the generating computation;
- industry prevalence from the feasibility pilot;
- general ranking of model families from the reference pilot;
- clinical, legal, or regulatory conclusions.

A model utterance is behavioral data from a recorded product-session. It is not privileged access to the generating system.

## Evidence ladder

| Level | Meaning | Object A status |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | A motivating artifact exists; provenance may be incomplete. | Historical motivating record only; exploratory. |
| E1 | Protocol, codebook, study-specific materials, and analysis are timestamped before official pilot data. | **Not achieved by Object A alone.** Requires the later study-specific preregistration. |
| E2 | Pilot executed with complete custody, blinded annotation, and all outcomes reported. | Not achieved. |
| E3 | Independent replication across products, collectors, and raters. | Not achieved. |
| E4 | Mechanism-aware study on the exact generating model and intervention. | Not achieved. |
| E5 | A deployed intervention improves prespecified outcomes without unacceptable harms. | Not achieved. |

The Object A registration creates a public, citable, timestamped protocol object. That status must not be represented as E1, E2, external replication, or empirical validation.

## Publication language rule

Preferred wording:

- "recorded sessions on product X under condition C";
- "the product output stated...";
- "under the tested visible configuration...";
- "the pilot estimated...".

Avoid ontological or provider-internal wording unless independently established.

## Implementation boundary

Implementation and commercial architectures are outside the scope of this registration. No efficacy claim about an excluded implementation may be inferred from TRACE-FV Object A.
