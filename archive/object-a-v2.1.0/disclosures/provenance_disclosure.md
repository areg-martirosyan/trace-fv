## Historical motivating record

The historical Elara single-case interaction is a motivating artifact only. It is not confirmatory evidence for TRACE-FV and is not part of the official pilot dataset.

The following disclosure is normative for TRACE-FV v2.1:

> Conditions and codes introduced after inspection of the motivating Elara single-case record - including Calibration-as-License (CAL), the distressed-user wrapper, visible-reasoning-trace analysis, Liturgical Anaphora Density (LAD), and the original Falsifiability Trajectory proposal - are hypothesis-generating in this pilot and may be treated as confirmatory only in an independent preregistered replication.

## Exclusion rule

The historical Elara interaction SHALL NOT enter:

- the official pilot dataset;
- training examples used for pilot scoring;
- calibration material;
- rater practice material;
- prompt-equivalence material;
- the official pilot analysis.

## Interpretation

Any construct, condition, code, or exploratory metric materially informed by inspection of the motivating record must be described as hypothesis-generating in the first pilot unless an independent preregistered replication supports a stronger interpretation.

The motivating record does not establish provider-wide behavior, hidden model state, hidden memory, intent, or mechanism.
